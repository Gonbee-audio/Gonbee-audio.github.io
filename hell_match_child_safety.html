Hell Match Child Safety Standards

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Hell Match is committed to maintaining a safe environment and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation of minors, or any content or behavior that endangers children.

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

Hell Match has a zero tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation. Users may not create, upload, share, request, promote, or distribute any content involving the sexual exploitation or abuse of minors.

2. Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Sexual content involving minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or sexual exploitation of minors
  • Requests to obtain, share, or distribute CSAM
  • Any behavior that puts children at risk of harm

3. User Reporting

Users can report child safety concerns, abusive behavior, inappropriate content, or suspected CSAE/CSAM through the in-app reporting feature or by contacting us directly at gonbee1230@gmail.com.

4. Review and Enforcement

Reports related to child safety are reviewed as a priority. If we identify content or behavior that violates this policy, we may remove the content, restrict or terminate user access, and take any other appropriate safety measures.

5. Reporting to Authorities

Where required by applicable laws and regulations, Hell Match will report suspected child sexual abuse material or child sexual exploitation to the appropriate authorities or designated organizations.

6. Compliance

Hell Match complies with applicable child safety laws and Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy for social and dating applications.
