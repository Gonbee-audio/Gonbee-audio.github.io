Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 11, 2026

Call/SMS Scam Guard Lite respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Call/SMS Scam Guard Lite does not automatically collect, read, or access your SMS messages, call history, contacts, phone state, or personal files.

The app only processes information that you manually enter or paste into the app, such as:

  • Suspicious SMS text
  • Phone numbers entered manually
  • Notes or history saved by the user

2. How We Use Information

The information you manually enter is used only to analyze possible scam risks and display safety suggestions.

For example, the app may check whether a message contains suspicious words, urgent payment requests, login warnings, suspicious links, or other common scam patterns.

3. Local Data Storage

Your check history may be saved locally on your device so that you can view previous results.

This data is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers.

You can delete your saved history from within the app at any time.

4. No Automatic SMS or Call Access

Call/SMS Scam Guard Lite does not use permissions to automatically read SMS messages, monitor incoming calls, access call logs, or access contacts.

The app works only with information that you manually provide.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of Call/SMS Scam Guard Lite does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or external AI services.

If third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Since the app does not send your manually entered content to any server, we do not have access to your scam check history.

7. Children’s Privacy

Call/SMS Scam Guard Lite is not intended to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Security

Because all check data is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for managing your device security.

Please avoid entering highly sensitive information such as passwords, PIN codes, bank account numbers, or credit card numbers into the app.

9. Disclaimer

Call/SMS Scam Guard Lite provides general scam risk guidance based on common scam patterns.

The app cannot guarantee that every scam will be detected, and it cannot confirm whether a phone number or message is truly safe.

Always verify suspicious messages or calls through official company websites, banks, or authorities.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
