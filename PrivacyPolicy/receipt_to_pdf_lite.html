Privacy Policy for receipt_to_pdf_lite

Effective date: June 13, 2026

```

receipt_to_pdf_lite is a lightweight mobile application designed to help users scan receipts, documents, and paper notes, then save them as PDF files.

This Privacy Policy explains how receipt_to_pdf_lite handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

receipt_to_pdf_lite does not require users to create an account.

The app does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, payment information, or other directly identifiable personal information.

The app may access or process the following information only when needed for app functionality:

  • Camera access: used to take photos of receipts, documents, or paper notes.
  • Local files created by the app: used to save scanned images and generated PDF files.
  • Recent file information: used to show recently created PDF files inside the app.
  • App usage and advertising-related data: may be processed by third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob.

2. Camera Permission

receipt_to_pdf_lite uses the camera only when you choose to scan a receipt, document, or paper note.

Photos taken inside the app are used to create PDF files. These images and PDF files are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or export them.

The app does not upload your scanned receipts, images, or PDF files to our servers.

3. Local Storage

receipt_to_pdf_lite may save generated PDF files and related recent file information on your device.

This information is stored locally and is used only to provide app features such as viewing, sharing, opening, or deleting created PDF files.

You can delete generated files from within the app or by using your device’s file management tools.

4. File Sharing

receipt_to_pdf_lite allows you to share generated PDF files through Android’s sharing feature.

When you choose to share a file, the selected third-party app or service may process that file according to its own privacy policy.

receipt_to_pdf_lite is not responsible for how third-party apps or services handle files after you choose to share them.

5. Advertising

receipt_to_pdf_lite may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, app interaction data, approximate location, and other data used to provide, measure, and improve advertisements.

Depending on your region, you may be asked to provide consent for personalized ads or related data processing.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit:
Google Privacy Policy
Google Advertising Policy

6. Third-Party Services

receipt_to_pdf_lite may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, app performance improvement, or related functionality.

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services used by the app may include:

  • Google AdMob

7. Data Sharing

receipt_to_pdf_lite does not sell your personal information.

The app does not share your scanned receipts, images, or generated PDF files with us.

Some advertising-related data may be shared with third-party advertising providers, such as Google AdMob, for the purpose of displaying, measuring, and improving ads.

8. Data Retention

Scanned images and generated PDF files are stored locally on your device.

receipt_to_pdf_lite does not store your scanned files on external servers.

You may delete locally stored files at any time.

Advertising-related data may be retained by third-party services according to their own policies.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Users are responsible for managing and protecting files stored on their own devices.
```
