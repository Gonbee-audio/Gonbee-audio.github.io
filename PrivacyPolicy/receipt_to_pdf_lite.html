Privacy Policy for receipt_to_pdf_lite

Effective date: June 13, 2026

receipt_to_pdf_lite is a mobile application designed to help users capture receipts and documents, recognize receipt information, manage expense records, and save or export information as PDF or CSV files.

This Privacy Policy explains how receipt_to_pdf_lite handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

receipt_to_pdf_lite does not require users to create an account.

We do not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, payment information, or other directly identifiable personal information through the app.

The app may access or process the following information when required to provide its features:

  • Camera data used to capture receipts, documents, or paper notes.
  • Receipt information such as merchant name, purchase date, total amount, and tax amount.
  • PDF files and receipt images created by the user.
  • Categories, notes, and expense records entered or generated within the app.
  • App usage, device, diagnostic, and advertising-related information that may be processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob.

2. Camera and Receipt Images

receipt_to_pdf_lite uses the device camera only when you choose to capture a receipt, document, or paper note.

Images captured through the app may be used to create PDF files and recognize receipt information.

Receipt images are not sent to our servers for text recognition.

3. Text Recognition

The app may recognize information from receipt images, including merchant name, purchase date, total amount, tax amount, and other receipt-related information.

Text recognition is performed on the user's device.

Receipt images and recognized fields are not uploaded to our servers for text recognition.

4. Local Storage

Receipt images, generated PDF files, recognized receipt fields, categories, notes, and expense records may be stored in the app's private storage on your device.

This information is used to provide app features such as viewing receipts, managing expense records, opening PDF files, sharing files, and exporting data.

receipt_to_pdf_lite does not require a user account and does not synchronize this information between devices through our servers.

5. Sharing and Export

receipt_to_pdf_lite allows users to share generated PDF files and export data, including CSV files.

When you choose to share or export information, the selected data is passed to a destination chosen by you through the standard sharing functionality provided by your device's operating system.

Any data received by a third-party application or service is handled according to that third party's own privacy policy.

6. Advertising

receipt_to_pdf_lite may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain information, including device information, advertising identifiers, app interaction data, approximate location, diagnostic information, and other information used to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

The specific information processed may depend on your device, operating system, region, consent choices, and advertising configuration.

Depending on your region, you may be asked to provide consent for personalized advertising or related data processing.

7. Third-Party Services

receipt_to_pdf_lite may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, performance monitoring, or related functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services used by the app may include:

  • Google AdMob

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not receive your receipt images, generated PDF files, recognized receipt fields, categories, notes, or expense records through our own servers as part of the app's normal receipt management functionality.

Certain advertising-related, device, usage, or diagnostic information may be processed by third-party providers such as Google AdMob in accordance with their privacy policies.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Receipt records, generated PDF files, recognized fields, categories, notes, and expense records are stored locally on your device.

When you delete a receipt record within the app, the associated structured record and related locally stored PDF file are removed from the app's storage where applicable.

Uninstalling the app generally removes information stored in the app's private storage, subject to the behavior of your device's operating system and any files you have separately exported or shared.

Information processed by third-party advertising or analytics services may be retained according to those services' own policies.

10. Children's Privacy

receipt_to_pdf_lite is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect directly identifiable personal information from children through our own servers.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information in connection with the app, please contact us.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for managing and protecting files they store, export, or share from their devices.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted with a revised effective date.
