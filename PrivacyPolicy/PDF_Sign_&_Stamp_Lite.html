Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application PDF Sign & Stamp Lite.

1. Information We Collect

PDF Sign & Stamp Lite does not require users to create an account. We do not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address.

2. PDF Files and Documents

PDF Sign & Stamp Lite allows users to select PDF files from their device in order to add signatures, text, dates, or stamps.

All PDF editing and processing is performed locally on the user's device. The app does not upload, transmit, or store your PDF files on any external server.

Edited PDF files are saved only to the location selected by the user.

3. File Access and Permissions

The app may use Android's file picker or document access system to allow users to select PDF files and save edited PDF files.

The app only accesses files that the user manually selects. The app does not request broad access to photos, videos, or media libraries.

4. Advertising

PDF Sign & Stamp Lite may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect certain information such as device information, advertising identifiers, app usage data, and approximate location data in order to provide, personalize, and improve advertising services.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Analytics and Crash Information

The app may use third-party services such as Google Play services, Google AdMob, or crash reporting tools to help improve app stability, performance, and user experience.

These services may collect diagnostic information, crash logs, device information, and app usage data.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent users' personal information.

Data may be processed by third-party service providers such as Google AdMob only as described in this Privacy Policy and in accordance with their own privacy policies.

7. Data Security

Since PDF files are processed locally on the user's device and are not uploaded to our servers, users remain in control of their documents.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

PDF Sign & Stamp Lite is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
