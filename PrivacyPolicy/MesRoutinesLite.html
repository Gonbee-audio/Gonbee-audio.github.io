Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mes Routines Lite handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Mes Routines Lite does not require users to create an account.

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, address, or payment information.

The app may store simple app-related data locally on your device, such as routines, habit names, checklist items, completion status, dates, preferences, or app settings. This information is used only to provide the app’s basic features.

2. How We Use Information

Information entered in the app is used only to provide app features, such as:

  • creating and managing daily routines;
  • checking completed tasks or habits;
  • showing daily progress;
  • saving simple app preferences;
  • improving the usability of the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Local Data Storage

Routine data and checklist information are stored locally on your device.

We do not operate a server for storing your routine data. If you delete the app, the locally stored data may also be deleted from your device.

4. Advertising

This app may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

These third-party services may collect and process certain information, such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interaction data, approximate location, crash information, diagnostics, and other technical data in order to provide, measure, and improve advertisements.

The collection and use of information by third-party advertising services are governed by their own privacy policies.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services by visiting:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Analytics and Diagnostics

Mes Routines Lite may use third-party services to collect anonymous or aggregated diagnostic information, such as crash reports, performance data, device type, operating system version, and app usage information.

This information helps us understand technical issues and improve the stability and quality of the app.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share the routine content you enter in the app with third parties.

However, third-party services such as advertising or diagnostic providers may process certain technical data as described in this Privacy Policy.

7. Data Retention

Data stored locally in the app remains on your device unless you delete it manually or uninstall the app.

Technical data processed by third-party services may be retained according to their own policies.

8. GDPR Privacy Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you may have certain rights under applicable data protection laws, including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data.

Since Mes Routines Lite does not require an account and does not directly collect personal information, most routine data is stored only on your device. You can delete this data by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

For information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob, please refer to the privacy settings and policies provided by those services.

9. Children’s Privacy

Mes Routines Lite is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
