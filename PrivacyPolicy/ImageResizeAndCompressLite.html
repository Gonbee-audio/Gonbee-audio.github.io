Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Image Resize & Compress Lite.

Image Resize & Compress Lite is designed to help users resize and compress images directly on their device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share personal information from users.

The app may allow users to select images from their device. These images are processed locally on the user's device and are not uploaded to any server.

Image Processing

All image resizing and compression operations are performed locally on the user's device. The app does not transmit selected images, edited images, or image metadata to external servers.

Permissions

The app may request access to images or media files on the user's device only for the purpose of selecting images to resize or compress.

The app does not access files without the user's action, and it does not collect or upload the selected images.

Advertising

This app may display advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, and usage data in order to provide and improve advertising services.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share users' personal information.

Since image processing is performed locally, selected images are not shared with us or with third parties by this app.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Security

We value user privacy and design the app to minimize data handling. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.
