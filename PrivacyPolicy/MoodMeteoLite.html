Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

Mood Meteo Lite is a simple mood journal app that allows users to record their daily mood using weather icons and short notes. This app is designed for personal use and does not require account registration or login.

Information We Collect

Mood Meteo Lite does not collect personally identifiable information from users.

The app may allow users to enter short notes and mood records. These records are stored locally on the user's device and are not sent to our servers.

Local Data Storage

Mood records, selected mood icons, dates, and notes are stored only on the user's device using local storage. The app does not upload, transmit, or share this data with any external server controlled by us.

Users can delete their mood records from within the app. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored data depending on the device settings.

Advertising

Mood Meteo Lite may display advertisements in the app. Advertisements may be provided by third-party advertising networks, such as Google AdMob. These third-party services may collect certain information, such as device identifiers, app activity, approximate location, crash logs, diagnostics, and advertising ID, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Users can manage or reset their advertising ID through their device settings.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services that may collect information used to identify users indirectly.

Third-party service providers may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

Please refer to the privacy policies of these third-party services for more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy & Security

Health and Medical Disclaimer

Mood Meteo Lite is not a medical, mental health, diagnostic, or treatment app. The app is intended only as a simple personal mood journal. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, therapy, or treatment.

If you have health or mental health concerns, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Children's Privacy

Mood Meteo Lite is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share users' personal mood records or notes. Locally stored journal data remains on the user's device unless the user chooses to share it outside the app.

Data Security

We value your privacy and use reasonable measures to protect app data. However, since mood records are stored locally on the user's device, users are responsible for managing access to their own device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
