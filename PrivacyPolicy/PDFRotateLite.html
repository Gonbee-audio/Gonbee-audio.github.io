Privacy Policy

PDF Rotate Lite is provided as a free app. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use this app.

Information Collection and Use

PDF Rotate Lite does not collect, store, or share any personal information directly.

The app allows users to select PDF files from their device in order to rotate pages and create a new PDF file. Selected PDF files are processed only for the purpose of providing the app's PDF rotation function.

The app does not upload your PDF files to any external server. Files are processed locally on your device whenever possible.

File Access

PDF Rotate Lite may ask you to select a PDF file using the Android system file picker. The app only accesses files that you explicitly choose.

The original PDF file is not modified. A new rotated PDF file may be created and saved or shared by the user.

Advertising

PDF Rotate Lite may display advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information such as device information, advertising identifiers, and usage data in order to provide and improve advertising services.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Third-Party Services

This app may use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

Third-party service providers used by the app may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

Data Sharing

PDF Rotate Lite does not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

However, third-party services such as Google AdMob may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We value your trust and aim to use commercially reasonable means to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

PDF Rotate Lite is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.
