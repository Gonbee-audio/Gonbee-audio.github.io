Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Warranty Tracker Lite handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Warranty Tracker Lite does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to any external server.

The app allows users to enter product warranty information such as product names, purchase dates, warranty expiration dates, store names, and notes. This information is stored locally on the user's device only.

2. Local Data Storage

All warranty records created in the app are saved locally on the user's device. We do not access, upload, share, or sell this data.

If the user deletes the app, locally stored data may also be deleted depending on the device and system behavior.

3. Photos and Media

If the app includes an optional photo attachment feature, photos selected by the user are used only within the app for warranty record management. These photos are stored locally on the user's device and are not uploaded to any external server.

4. Permissions

Warranty Tracker Lite may request access to photos or media only when the user chooses to attach an image to a warranty record. The app does not access photos or media without the user's action.

5. Third-Party Services

Warranty Tracker Lite does not use third-party analytics services.

If advertising services such as Google AdMob are added in the future, the app may use third-party advertising services that may collect certain device or usage information according to their own privacy policies. Warranty information entered by the user will not be shared with advertising services.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer user data to third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

Warranty Tracker Lite is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect personal information, no personal data from children is knowingly collected.

8. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on the user's device, users are responsible for managing their device security, backups, and app data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
