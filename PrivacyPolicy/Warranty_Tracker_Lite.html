Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Warranty Tracker Lite (also published as My Warranty File on the App Store) handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Warranty Tracker Lite does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to any external server operated by us.

The app allows users to enter product warranty information such as product names, purchase dates, warranty expiration dates, store names, and notes. This information is stored locally on the user's device only, and is never sent to us or to any server we control.

2. Local Data Storage

All warranty records created in the app are saved locally on the user's device. We do not access, upload, share, or sell this data.

If the user deletes the app, locally stored data may also be deleted depending on the device and system behavior.

3. Photos and Media

If the app includes an optional photo attachment feature, photos selected by the user are used only within the app for warranty record management. These photos are stored locally on the user's device and are not uploaded to any external server.

4. Permissions

Warranty Tracker Lite may request access to photos or media only when the user chooses to attach an image to a warranty record. The app does not access photos or media without the user's action.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Warranty Tracker Lite displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. To serve ads, including personalized ads where permitted, Google and its partners may collect and use device and usage information such as advertising identifiers (e.g. IDFA), IP address, general device information, and app interaction data, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

Warranty information entered by the user (product names, purchase dates, warranty details, notes, and any attached photos) is never shared with Google AdMob or any other advertising service.

On iOS, the app requests App Tracking Transparency permission where required before using identifiers for personalized advertising. You can manage ad tracking permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

6. Third-Party Services

Aside from Google AdMob as described above, Warranty Tracker Lite does not use third-party analytics services.

7. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer user-entered warranty data to third parties. Advertising-related data is collected and processed directly by Google AdMob as described in Section 5.

8. Children's Privacy

Warranty Tracker Lite is not intended for use by children and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app itself does not collect personal information, no personal data from children is knowingly collected by us; advertising services may be subject to their own child-directed treatment as configured by the developer.

9. Data Security

Since all warranty data is stored locally on the user's device, users are responsible for managing their device security, backups, and app data.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
