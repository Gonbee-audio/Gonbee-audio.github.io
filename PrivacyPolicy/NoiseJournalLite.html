This Privacy Policy explains how Noise Journal Lite handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Noise Journal Lite does not require users to create an account.

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, address, payment information, or contact information.

The app may store simple app-related data locally on your device, such as noise notes, mood records, tags, preferences, or app settings, only to provide the app’s basic features.

Noise Journal Lite does not upload your journal records to our own server.

2. How We Use Information

Any information entered in the app is used only to provide app features, such as:

  • saving noise or mood records;
  • helping you review your daily sound environment;
  • saving simple preferences or app settings;
  • improving the usability of the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Local Storage

Noise Journal Lite is designed to work mainly with local data stored on your device. Records, notes, preferences, and settings may remain on your device unless you delete them or uninstall the app.

If you uninstall the app, locally stored data may be deleted depending on your device and operating system settings.

The handling of information by third-party advertising services is governed by their own privacy policies. You can learn more about how Google uses data here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not share personal information directly collected by us because we do not directly collect personal information.

However, third-party services used in the app, such as advertising or diagnostics services, may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.
