Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Meter Reading Lite is a simple offline app for recording electricity, gas, and water meter readings. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Meter Reading Lite does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app allows users to enter meter readings, dates, and optional notes. This data is stored locally on the user's device and is not sent to any external server.

2. Local Data Storage

All meter reading data is stored only on your device. The developer does not have access to this data.

If you uninstall the app, the locally stored data may be deleted from your device.

3. Permissions

Meter Reading Lite does not require camera, location, contacts, microphone, photo, or file access permissions.

4. Internet and External Services

Meter Reading Lite does not require an internet connection for its main features. The app does not use external APIs, analytics services, or user account systems.

5. Advertising

If advertisements are added in the future, third-party advertising providers may collect certain information in accordance with their own privacy policies. Any such change will be reflected in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share user data with third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

Meter Reading Lite is not directed to children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
