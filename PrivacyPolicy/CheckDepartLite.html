This Privacy Policy explains how Check Départ Lite handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. App Overview

Check Départ Lite is a simple checklist application designed to help users check their belongings and home before leaving. Users can manage items such as keys, wallet, phone, medicine, documents, door lock, windows, gas, and lights.

The app is designed to work locally on your device. It does not require account registration and does not use a server to store checklist data.

2. Information We Collect

Check Départ Lite does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, address, payment information, or login information.

The app may store checklist data locally on your device, including:

  • Checklist item names
  • Checklist categories
  • Checked or unchecked status
  • Custom items added by the user
  • App settings

This information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to our server.

3. How We Use Information

Locally stored data is used only to provide the main features of the app, including displaying your checklist, saving checked states, adding or editing items, deleting items, using templates, and resetting checklist data.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

4. Local Data Storage

Check Départ Lite stores app data locally on your device using local storage mechanisms such as SharedPreferences.

The data remains on your device unless you delete it by using the reset option in the app, clearing app data from your device settings, or uninstalling the app.

5. Account Registration

Check Départ Lite does not require users to create an account. The app does not provide login, user registration, or cloud synchronization features.

6. Internet Connection and Server Communication

The core features of Check Départ Lite do not require server communication. Checklist data is not uploaded to an external server.

7. Advertising

Check Départ Lite may display advertisements in the future using third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

If advertisements are enabled in a future version, third-party advertising providers may collect and process certain data such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interactions, approximate location, and diagnostic information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

At the current stage, the app may include an advertising placeholder area. If advertising is added, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

8. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services for app distribution or advertising. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services that may be used include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

9. Data Sharing

We do not share your checklist data with external servers or third parties.

If third-party advertising services are added in the future, those services may process certain data independently as described in their own privacy policies.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Checklist data is stored locally on your device until you delete it.

You can delete your data by:

  • Using the reset option inside the app settings
  • Clearing the app data from your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app

11. Children’s Privacy

Check Départ Lite is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Security

We aim to keep the app simple and secure by storing checklist data locally on the user’s device and avoiding unnecessary data transmission.

However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own devices.

13. International Users and GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you may have certain rights under applicable data protection laws, including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data.

Since Check Départ Lite does not require an account and does not directly collect personal information, most app data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by resetting the app data or uninstalling the app.
