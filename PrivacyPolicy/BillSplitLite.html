Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Bill Split Lite provided by the developer.

Bill Split Lite is a simple bill splitting and expense calculation app. The app is designed to help users calculate shared payments, tips, and amounts per person.

Information We Collect

Bill Split Lite does not require user registration, login, or account creation.

The app may allow users to enter information such as total amount, number of people, tip rate, currency, memo, and calculation history. This information is stored locally on the user's device only.

We do not collect, transmit, or store this information on external servers.

Local Data Storage

Bill Split Lite may store calculation history and app settings locally on the user's device using local storage. This data is used only to provide app functionality, such as displaying past bill splitting records and saving default settings.

Users can delete saved history within the app at any time.

Advertising

Bill Split Lite may display advertisements through third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

These advertising services may collect and use certain information such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and app usage data in order to provide, personalize, and measure ads.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies. These services may collect information as described in their respective privacy policies.

Third-party service providers may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

Permissions

Bill Split Lite does not require access to sensitive device permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location.

If advertisements are enabled, network access may be used to load ads.

Children's Privacy

Bill Split Lite is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Data Security

Since calculation history and settings are stored locally on the user's device, users are responsible for managing access to their own device.

We do not operate external servers for storing bill splitting records or calculation history.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Users are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
