Privacy Policy

App: 1 Minute Workout Daily

Last updated: July 29, 2026

1

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how 1 Minute Workout Daily (the “App”) handles information when you use the App.

The App is designed to provide simple workout guidance and does not require users to create an account.

2

Information We Handle

Information stored on your device

Workout-related settings or records may be stored locally on your device. The App developer does not directly receive or store this locally saved information on an external server.

Information processed by third-party services

The App uses third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob. These services may automatically process limited technical information, such as:

  • Device and operating system information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • App interactions and advertising performance data
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash or error data

The handling of information by Google is governed by Google’s own privacy policies and controls.

3

How Information Is Used

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the App
  • To display and measure advertisements
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve performance
  • To protect the App against fraud, misuse, or security threats
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

The App developer does not sell personal information directly collected from users.

4

Data Retention

The App does not require users to create an account and does not directly store personal information or workout records on servers operated by the App developer.

Information stored locally on the user’s device remains on the device until the user deletes the information, clears the App’s data, or uninstalls the App.

Limited technical information, such as device information, usage data, crash logs, and advertising-related information, may be processed by third-party service providers used by the App.

Such information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, analyze technical issues, improve the App, comply with legal obligations, and prevent fraud or misuse.

The specific retention periods for information processed by third-party service providers are determined by those providers in accordance with their respective privacy policies and legal obligations.

Uninstalling the App will remove data stored locally by the App from the device. However, uninstalling the App may not automatically delete information previously processed by third-party service providers.
5

When submitting a deletion request, please include the following information:

  • The name of the App: 1 Minute Workout Daily.
  • A statement that you are requesting deletion of your data.

We will review and respond to valid deletion requests within a reasonable period. We may request additional information when necessary to verify the request and identify the relevant data.

Because the App does not require a user account and does not directly maintain a database of personal user profiles, we may not be able to identify or delete data that cannot reasonably be linked to a specific user.

Information processed independently by third-party service providers, including Google AdMob, may be subject to the provider’s own deletion, retention, and privacy controls. Users may also manage advertising and privacy settings through their device settings and Google account settings.

Certain information may be retained when required by law, necessary to protect legal rights, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or comply with regulatory obligations.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children under the age required by applicable law to consent to the processing of personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
