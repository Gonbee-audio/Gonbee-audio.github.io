Privacy Policy

Aujourd’hui – Smart Food Balance

1. Introduction

Aujourd’hui (“the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

The App may process the following information when you use it:

  • Text input describing meals or food choices
  • Selections made during onboarding, such as lifestyle preferences, usage goals, and country or region

This information is not stored permanently. Meal descriptions and inputs are used only to generate on-the-spot guidance and are not saved to any database or user profile.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

The App may automatically collect limited technical information, including:

  • App usage data, such as crashes or basic interaction events
  • Device information, including operating system and app version

This information is used solely to improve app stability, performance, and reliability.

3. Data Storage and Retention

  • The App does not store meal logs, meal history, or health records.
  • No calorie tracking or nutritional history is saved.
  • User inputs cannot be reviewed, restored, or accessed later.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

The App offers optional premium features via subscription.

  • Payments are processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
  • The App does not collect or store credit card or payment details.
  • Only subscription status, such as free or premium, is used within the App to enable features.

5. Use of AI Features

The App may use AI technologies to generate guidance based on user input.

  • AI processing is used only for temporary analysis and response generation.
  • Inputs are not stored, reused, or associated with identifiable user profiles.
  • AI usage is limited to providing immediate, contextual guidance.

6. Sharing of Information

The App does not sell, rent, or share user information with third parties, except when required by law or necessary to protect the security and integrity of the App.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are implemented to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not designed for children. If a minor uses the App, it should be with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will become effective once published within the App or on the official distribution page.
