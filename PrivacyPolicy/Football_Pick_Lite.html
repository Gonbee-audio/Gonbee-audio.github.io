Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 13, 2026

Football Pick Lite is a simple football prediction note app developed by gonbee1230. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Football Pick Lite does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The app allows users to enter football match prediction data, such as team names, predicted scores, actual scores, match dates, and optional competition names. This information is stored locally on the user's device.

2. Local Data Storage

Match prediction data entered by the user is stored locally on the device. This data is not sent to our servers, and we do not have access to it.

If the user deletes the app, locally stored data may also be deleted depending on the device settings and operating system behavior.

3. Sharing Function

Football Pick Lite may allow users to share their football prediction text or prediction card through other apps installed on their device.

Sharing is controlled by the user. The app does not automatically share any data. Once information is shared through another app, it is handled according to the privacy policy of that third-party app.

4. Advertising

Football Pick Lite may display advertisements in the future using third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

These advertising services may collect and use certain information, such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app usage data, and approximate location, in order to provide, measure, and improve ads.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Analytics

Football Pick Lite does not currently use analytics services.

If analytics services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Permissions

Football Pick Lite is designed to work with minimal permissions.

The app may use sharing-related functions provided by the device operating system when the user chooses to share prediction content. The app does not access contacts, call logs, SMS, camera, microphone, or precise location.

7. Children’s Privacy

Football Pick Lite is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

8. GDPR and EEA Users

For users in the European Economic Area, the app is designed to minimize data collection. Match prediction data is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to our servers.

If third-party advertising services are used, users may be asked for consent where required by applicable law. Users may also be able to manage advertising preferences through their device settings.

9. Third-Party Services

Football Pick Lite may use third-party services in the future, including but not limited to:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

10. Data Security

Since user prediction data is stored locally on the device, users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device.

We do not operate a server that stores user prediction data.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
