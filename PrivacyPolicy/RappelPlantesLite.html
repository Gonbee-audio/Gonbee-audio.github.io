Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rappel Plantes Lite handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Rappel Plantes Lite does not require users to create an account.

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, address, payment information, photos, or precise location.

The app may store simple plant-related information locally on your device, such as:

  • plant names;
  • plant categories;
  • watering frequency;
  • last watering date;
  • notes entered by the user;
  • app settings.

This information is stored on your device only and is used to provide the basic features of the app.

2. How We Use Information

Any information entered in the app is used only to provide app features, such as:

  • displaying your plant list;
  • calculating the next watering date;
  • showing watering reminders or status labels;
  • saving your plant notes and preferences locally;
  • improving the usability of the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Local Storage

Rappel Plantes Lite stores plant information locally on your device. This data is not sent to our servers.

If you uninstall the app or clear the app data, the locally stored information may be deleted.

4. Advertising

This app may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

These third-party services may collect and process certain information, such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interaction data, approximate location, and diagnostic information, in order to provide, measure, and improve advertisements.

The handling of such data is governed by the privacy policies of the third-party advertising providers.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services by visiting:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Analytics

Rappel Plantes Lite does not directly use analytics services to track users.

However, third-party services such as advertising SDKs or app store services may automatically collect limited technical information for security, diagnostics, performance measurement, or advertising purposes.

6. Permissions

Rappel Plantes Lite is designed to work without requiring access to sensitive device permissions such as camera, photos, contacts, microphone, or precise location.

If future versions add optional features that require permissions, we will explain why those permissions are needed.

7. Data Sharing

We do not share the plant information you enter in the app with third parties.

Third-party advertising providers may process certain technical or advertising-related data as described in the Advertising section.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Plant information entered in the app remains stored locally on your device until you delete it, clear the app data, or uninstall the app.

You can delete individual plant records from within the app at any time.

9. Children’s Privacy

Rappel Plantes Lite is not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

10. GDPR and EEA Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you may have certain rights under applicable data protection laws, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data.

Since Rappel Plantes Lite stores user-entered plant information locally on your device and does not directly collect personal information on our servers, most data can be managed directly within the app or by deleting the app data from your device.

For data processed by third-party advertising providers, please refer to their respective privacy policies and consent management options.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to keep the app simple and avoid unnecessary data collection. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
