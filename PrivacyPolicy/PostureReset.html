Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Posture_Reset is an app designed to help users improve posture awareness and build simple stretching or posture reset habits. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user information.

Information We Collect

Posture_Reset does not require users to create an account.

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as name, email address, phone number, address, or precise location.

The app may store simple app-related data on the user’s device, such as posture reminder settings, usage preferences, and app configuration. This information is stored locally on the device and is not sent to our servers.

How We Use Information

Any information stored locally is used only to provide the app’s core features, such as:

  • Saving reminder settings
  • Keeping user preferences
  • Improving the in-app experience

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Third-Party Services

Posture_Reset may use third-party services provided by Google Play or the device operating system for app distribution, security, crash reporting, or basic performance monitoring.

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Storage and Security

App settings and preferences may be stored locally on the user’s device.

Because we do not operate a user account system or collect personal data on our own servers, we do not store personal user data externally.

Children’s Privacy

Posture_Reset is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
