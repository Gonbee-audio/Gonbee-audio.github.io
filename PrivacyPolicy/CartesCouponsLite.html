Privacy Policy

App Name: Cartes & Coupons Lite

Developer: Gonbee

Effective Date: June 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Cartes & Coupons Lite handles user information. Cartes & Coupons Lite is a simple app for managing loyalty cards, coupons, membership numbers, expiration dates, and related notes.

2. Information We Collect

Cartes & Coupons Lite does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or any external server.

The app allows users to enter information such as store names, card or coupon names, membership numbers, coupon codes, expiration dates, and notes. This information is stored locally on the user's device only.

3. Local Data Storage

All data entered in the app is stored locally on the user's device using local storage. The developer does not have access to this data.

If the user deletes the app, the locally stored data may also be deleted from the device.

4. Internet Access and External Servers

Cartes & Coupons Lite does not require account registration and does not send user-created card, coupon, or note data to any external server.

5. Permissions

Cartes & Coupons Lite does not require camera permission, location permission, contacts permission, or microphone permission.

6. Advertising

Cartes & Coupons Lite may display advertisements in the future. If advertising is implemented, third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob may collect certain information, such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interaction data, crash logs, diagnostics, and approximate location, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

The app itself does not sell personal information.

7. Analytics

Cartes & Coupons Lite does not currently use analytics services to track user behavior. If analytics services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Children’s Privacy

Cartes & Coupons Lite is not directed to children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data Security

Since all user-entered data is stored locally on the device, users are responsible for managing and protecting their own device. Please avoid entering highly sensitive information such as passwords, bank card PINs, or confidential personal identification numbers.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
